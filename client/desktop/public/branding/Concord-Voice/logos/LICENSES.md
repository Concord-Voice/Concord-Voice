# Logo Licenses — `client/desktop/public/branding/Concord-Voice/logos/`

This directory ships Concord Voice's own brand marks used by the `client/desktop`
Electron client. They are self-hosted (served from the packaged bundle / renderer
`public/`) — never loaded from a CDN, per the no-CDN privacy stance.
